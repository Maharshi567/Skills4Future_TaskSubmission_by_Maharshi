PROJECT TITLE

Smart Energy Consumption Analyzer

Name: Maharshi Danidhariya

PROBLEM STATEMENT

Many households and organizations are unaware of their energy consumption patterns, leading to excessive electricity usage, higher electricity bills, and increased carbon emissions.

An intelligent system is needed to analyze historical energy consumption data, predict future energy usage, and provide recommendations for reducing energy consumption and promoting sustainable living.

OBJECTIVE

1. Analyze electricity consumption data.
2. Identify historical energy usage patterns.
3. Predict future energy consumption using Machine Learning.
4. Estimate future electricity costs.
5. Generate energy-saving recommendations.
6. Promote sustainable energy usage and reduce carbon footprint.

--------------------------------------------------

EXPECTED IMPACT

- Reduce electricity wastage.
- Lower electricity bills.
- Increase awareness of sustainable energy usage.
- Reduce carbon emissions.
- Support green living and environmental sustainability.

--------------------------------------------------

SDGs SUPPORTED

SDG 7 – Affordable and Clean Energy

SDG 12 – Responsible Consumption and Production

SDG 13 – Climate Action

SDG 17 – Partnerships for the Goals

--------------------------------------------------

CONCLUSION

The Smart Energy Consumption Analyzer uses Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning to analyze historical electricity consumption data and predict future energy usage. By providing accurate forecasts and actionable energy-saving recommendations, the system helps individuals and organizations reduce energy wastage, lower electricity costs, and contribute to a more sustainable future.
